Privacy Policy

Anubis is a single-user personal assistant application. Its Google account connection is used only to read and send email from its own account, and to create and organize Google Docs and Drive files on behalf of its one owner.

No data accessed through this connection is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party. Nothing is used for advertising. Access is limited to files and messages this application's own account creates or receives — never a broader account.
